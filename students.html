PRAYAS · Student FAQs

Show all answers
Who can apply?
ITI graduates, diploma holders and engineering graduates who want a career in manufacturing and automation. Women are warmly encouraged to apply.
Do I need prior industrial experience?
No. Most students join straight from ITI or diploma college.
Will there be hands-on learning?
Yes. Most of your time is practical work on live panels, PLCs, drives and automation rigs.
Which pathway is right for me?
A PRAYAS career advisor reviews your qualification and goals and recommends the right one.
Do companies hire from PRAYAS?
Yes. Graduates are introduced to partner manufacturers through our placement drives.
Is placement guaranteed?
We support every learner with interview preparation and employer introductions. Placement depends on completing the programme and meeting placement requirements.
