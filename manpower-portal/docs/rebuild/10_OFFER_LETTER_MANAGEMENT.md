## 1. Offer Letter Architecture

Formal appointment letters are generated by `PipelineController::createAndReleaseOffer()` and stored in `offer_letters`.

### Reference Number Taxonomy:
Every offer letter receives an immutable, human-readable reference number:
$$\text{Ref: } \mathbf{OFFER\text{-}YYYYMM\text{-}XXXX}$$
*(where $YYYYMM$ represents current year and month, and $XXXX$ represents the zero-padded selection ID).*

---

## 2. Offer Dossier Components

The printable offer letter template incorporates:
1. **Employer & Academy Branding**: Company Name, Plant Unit, CIN/Registration, partnership acknowledgement with PRAYAS – The Automation Academy.
2. **Candidate Credentials**: Candidate Name (masked in industry view until release), ITI Trade, Batch Year, Reference ID.
3. **Core Remuneration Package**: Monthly Gross CTC, Salary Type (`Monthly Gross`, `Stipend`, `Hourly`), Statutory Benefits (PF, ESIC, Industrial Transport, Subsidized Canteen, Medical & PPE).
4. **Dates & Deadlines**: Formal Issue Date, Offer Acceptance Validity Date (default 7 days), Proposed Physical Reporting Date.
5. **Standard Terms of Employment**: Six-month probation period, certificate verification contingency, and factory safety compliance.
6. **Executive Signatory Block**: Authorized HR Signatory and candidate physical/digital acknowledgement line.

---

## 3. Candidate Offer Response Tracking

Governed by `PipelineController::recordOfferResponse()`:
- `Accepted`: Advances candidate to `Offer Accepted` (and queues for `Joining Pending`).
- `Declined`: Records candidate decline rationale and updates candidate match stage to `Offer Declined`.
