## 1. Assessment Framework Overview

For specialized automated manufacturing operations, candidates undergo dual-track aptitude and vocational trade assessments:

1. **Aptitude Assessment**:
   - Numerical reasoning and basic industrial mathematics.
   - Spatial visualization, schematic interpretation, and blueprint orientation.
   - Pattern recognition and machine component logic.

2. **Technical & Vocational Assessment**:
   - Trade-specific theory (Ohm's Law, PLC wiring, machining tolerances, welding joint specifications).
   - Workshop measurements (Vernier caliper, micrometer, multimeter diagnostics).
   - Workshop health, safety, and hazardous material protocols.

---

## 2. Assessment Integration in Candidate Pipeline

Assessment scores are factored into:
- Initial Algorithmic Matching (`trade_score`, `skills_score`).
- Recruiter evaluation prior to client release.
- Technical round evaluation feedback forms (`technical_rating`, `trade_knowledge_rating`).
- Overall Candidate Evaluation Dossier submitted to industry hiring managers.
