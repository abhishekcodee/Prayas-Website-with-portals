Privacy Policy

How we protect and handle your personal data across PRAYAS programs.

1. Introduction

PRAYAS — The Automation Academy ("PRAYAS", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy informs you how we look after your personal data when you visit our website, submit recruitment enquiries, or register for our workforce readiness and technical training programs.

2. Information We Collect

We collect and process personal data in connection with your use of our platform, including:

  • Candidate Data: Name, mobile number, email, address, educational background, technical skills, certifications, and employment history.
  • Recruitment Data: Assessment scores, video introductions, webcam verification photographs, and resume submissions.
  • Employer & Partner Data: Organization name, recruiter contact details, hiring requirements, and collaboration enquiries.
  • Technical & Usage Data: IP address, browser type, device information, and interaction timestamps.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process your information for legitimate educational, training, and recruitment purposes, including:

  • Processing student admissions and evaluating suitability for training batches.
  • Facilitating employer recruitment and interview scheduling with partner manufacturers.
  • Responding to enquiries submitted via website contact forms.
  • Maintaining internal administrative and academic records.
  • Complying with statutory laws and industrial safety standards.

4. Data Security & Retention

We implement strict administrative, technical, and physical safeguards—including encrypted database storage, secure sessions, and restricted administrative privileges—to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure. We retain candidate records only for as long as necessary to fulfill the training and recruitment objectives.

5. Contact Information

For questions or requests concerning your data under this policy, please reach out to our team at info@prayas-academy.com or call +91 9372931129.
